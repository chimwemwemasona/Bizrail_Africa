## Research pilot

- [ ] Recruit the proposed 150-business Lusaka/Copperbelt cohort: 50 retail/hardware/parts, 40 wholesalers/distributors, 30 service SMEs and 30 light manufacturers with simple stock needs.
- [ ] Roll out progressively; validate sales, purchases, stock, cash/bank/mobile reconciliation, accountant portal and financing profile before advancing each cohort.
- [ ] Measure active businesses, data completeness, stock accuracy, sync conflicts, fiscal success/unknowns, time to reconcile, paid conversion and support burden.
- [ ] Record actual results separately from targets; do not substitute the old three-anchor/500-buyer plan.
- [ ] Decide readiness for the proposed 1,000-business launch and financing-data experiment based on evidence.

## Expansion boundary

- [ ] Scope release two separately: multi-branch, supplier purchase orders, payment links/initiation, payroll connectors, formalisation workflows, lender APIs and industry modules.
- [ ] Keep own-balance-sheet lending, proprietary wallets and custody outside the product unless a separately approved strategy changes this.
- [ ] Preserve Ubuntu Hetzner and non-S3 storage constraints in future infrastructure decisions.

The research horizons are months 0–3 discovery/foundation, months 4–6 closed beta, months 7–9 pilot and months 10–12 launch. They are planning assumptions, not promised dates.
